Justin Tung // GTM Systems // San Francisco

I build the systems behind go-to-market — revenue ops, sales and marketing process, and the foundational systems work that ties it all together.

I'm Justin — founder of Justin Tung Consulting, a GTM systems practice based in San Francisco. I help startups and growing teams design the systems behind their go-to-market: revenue operations, sales and marketing process, and the Salesforce platform work that ties it all together.

I tend to be the person who shows up before the system exists. First business systems analyst at Airbnb, where I grew the team and helped land the China office's IT footprint. First recurring-revenue product at Ramp (Plus). First B2C mobile platform on Salesforce Communities + Mobile Publisher at GuideTime. The pattern keeps repeating, which I take to mean it's the work I'm actually built for. Before all of that, F500 Salesforce implementations at Bluewolf, 13 Salesforce certifications somewhere along the way, and a Berkeley M.E.T. (Management, Entrepreneurship & Technology) plus Econ degree that drilled the wiring between technical and commercial.

Coming to the US as an immigrant at 12, I've spent years working through imposter syndrome and cultural assimilation. I've worked across China, India, Singapore, Japan, Ireland, and the UK; I get by in Mandarin, Portuguese, and Spanish. What's stuck: happiness comes from exploring without fear, enjoying without guilt, and taking time to celebrate the milestones along the way.

Lately I've been deep on how AI tooling — Claude Code in particular — reshapes the shape of back-office and GTM work.
